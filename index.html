Self-Paced Teaching Guides

Everything traces back to one hub: DTC's book-entry settlement system

At the center is the Depository Trust Company and its nominee, Cede & Co. — the mechanism that holds nearly all U.S. securities in electronic form and settles trades between them. This collection starts there, then follows how corporations, non-profits, and trusts each interact with that system for eligibility, custody, and titling, and how securities held in it get pledged as collateral under UCC Article 8. Two related guides round out the picture: a Treasury bill of exchange in trade finance, and an SF 28 surety bond affidavit in federal contracting — different instruments, but the same underlying discipline of verifying a claim before a transaction goes through. Each guide pairs a plain-language walkthrough with a clickable concept map, sourced definitions, and a short self-graded quiz.

Teaching Materials

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand DTC eligibility requirements, the FAST program, and the submission process through a sponsoring participant
  • Master international trade payments, documentary collections, and bills of exchange under UCP 600 and URC 522
  • Navigate federal surety requirements (SF 28) and FAR 28.203 compliance for government contracting
  • Explore the U.S. securities infrastructure: DTC, DTCC, NSCC, FICC, Cede & Co., and cross-border links to Euroclear
  • Understand the T+1 settlement cycle implemented May 28, 2024, and its impact on fails, funding, and FX hedging
  • Distinguish trust structures and fiduciary duties in securities titling and nominee arrangements
  • Collateral: Master the legal mechanics of pledging book-entry securities as collateral under UCC Article 8, including Account Control Agreements (ACAs), margin lending, and rehypothecation
  • Corporate actions: Follow how dividends, splits, tenders, and votes pass from the issuer through Cede & Co. and DTC participants to beneficial owners—including omnibus proxy basics
  • Transfer agents: Operate the issuer–DTC interface—FAST balances, DWAC movements, DRS vs street name, and restriction rejects
  • Lending & repo: Move inventory for shorts/fails and finance cash against securities on the same book-entry pipes

SIFMU · Clearing · Custody

DTC

The Depository Trust Company — the world's largest central securities depository. Holds securities in book-entry form through its nominee, Cede & Co. Industry-owned, regulated by the SEC.

SIFMU · CSD · Book-entry

NSCC

National Securities Clearing Corporation. Provides centralized clearing, risk management, and netting for equities and corporate debt. Operates the Continuous Net Settlement (CNS) system.

CCP · Netting · Equities

FICC

Fixed Income Clearing Corporation. Clears and settles U.S. government securities, mortgage-backed securities, and repo transactions through the GSD and MBSD divisions.

Repo · Treasuries · MBS

Euroclear

International central securities depository (ICSD) based in Brussels. Settles cross-border trades via the Bridge to Clearstream and links to 50+ local CSDs. Handles Eurobonds and domestic securities.

ICSD · Bridge · Cross-border

Cede & Co.

DTC's partnership nominee. All securities deposited at DTC are registered in the name of Cede & Co. The issuer and trustee know only this nominee; beneficial ownership exists solely on intermediary books.

Nominee · Record holder · DTC

DTCC

The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation. Parent company of DTC, NSCC, and FICC. Clears and settles nearly all equity, debt, and asset-backed transactions in the United States.

Holding Company · Parent
